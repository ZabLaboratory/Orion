## Summary

`Lumencast/lumencast-go` merged `0c7cfc6` (PR#22, issue#21): `protocol.Snapshot`
now carries the same six additive `omitempty` projection-metadata fields
`Delta` already had, and the kit's `Scene` remembers the metadata of the most
recent `EmitWithCauseAndMetadata` call (`lastMetadata`) and stamps it onto
every `Snapshot` it later constructs — `Set`/`refreshAll`, late-join
subscribe, backpressure collapse, and live scene migration.

This bumps Orion's pin to that commit and repairs
`orion_lsdp_snapshot_identity_gap_total` accordingly. Before the bump, the
counter had one job: flag that Orion was about to drop a known projection
identity because `protocol.Snapshot` (the kit's wire type) had no field for
it — a real, unavoidable loss on every bootstrap/scene-switch reseed. After
the bump that's no longer true: `sceneMirror.recordIdentity` and the kit's
`Scene.lastMetadata` are updated on the *exact same*
`EmitWithCauseAndMetadata` call, against the *exact same* `*lserver.Scene`
(`internal/lsdp/mirror.go`), so a known identity is now structurally
guaranteed to ride the Snapshot frame. Leaving the counter incrementing in
that branch would make it a permanent false positive from the moment this PR
merges — "a counter that cries wolf after the fix is worse than no counter."

The metric, the `SnapshotMetrics` interface, and the `SetSnapshotMetrics`
wiring in `cmd/orion/main.go` are **kept, not deleted** — they're documented
as a regression guard: if a future change desynchronizes the mirror's own
bookkeeping from the kit scene's state (e.g. a mirror rebound to a
persistent scene without replaying its Delta history), this is exactly the
kind of bug the seam exists to catch. The "nothing known yet" branch
(legitimate omission, not a loss) is untouched.

## Changes

- `go.mod`/`go.sum`: `github.com/Lumencast/lumencast-go` bumped to
  `v0.3.2-0.20260816093300-0c7cfc65c694` (pseudo-version resolving `0c7cfc6`;
  no tag exists yet at that commit — verified via `go get @0c7cfc6`, not
  guessed).
- `internal/lsdp/mirror.go`: `observeSnapshotIdentityGap` no longer
  increments `SnapshotIdentityGap` when an identity is known — downgraded
  from Warn+Inc to Info-only, with doc explaining the structural guarantee.
  `sceneMirror`'s `identMu`/`lastIdentity`/`hasIdentity` doc updated to match.
- `internal/lsdp/wire.go`: `snapshotMetrics` field and `SnapshotMetrics`
  interface doc rewritten to describe the regression-guard posture.
- `internal/obs/metrics.go`: `LSDPSnapshotIdentityGap` comment updated
  (expected to report zero under current code; kept as a guard).
- `internal/lsdp/snapshot_identity_gap_test.go`: the two "known identity"
  assertions rewritten (see below) plus one new end-to-end test:
  `TestLSDP_SnapshotForwardCarriesKnownProjectionIdentity` dials a real LSDP
  WebSocket subscriber, forwards a Delta carrying an identity, then forwards
  a Snapshot, and asserts the **actual frame received over the wire** carries
  all six fields — proof at the wire level, not just mirror-internal
  bookkeeping.

## Écarts / hypothèses

- I renamed/rewrote (not silently adjusted) the two existing
  `SnapshotIdentityGap` tests that asserted the OLD (now-wrong) semantics —
  each has a doc comment explaining exactly why the assertion flipped. The
  "nothing known yet" test is untouched.
- The `SnapshotMetrics` seam now has zero live call sites that actually
  increment it under current code (proven by the invariant above). I kept
  it wired rather than removing `SetSnapshotMetrics` from `cmd/orion/main.go`
  or deleting the interface — per the bail's explicit instruction not to
  delete the counter. If the team would rather retire the seam outright,
  that's a follow-up, not something I decided unilaterally here.
- `internal/runtime` (Engine A) untouched, as instructed — Orion's own
  `protocol.Snapshot`/`Delta` internal types are unrelated to the kit's wire
  types and out of scope.
